# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 July 2026

Côte Tennis helps adults find tennis partners. This document is a product-ready draft and must be reviewed with the operator’s legal identity and contact details before public release.

## Data we collect

- Phone number for authentication. It is never shown to other players.
- Profile information: first name, birth year, optional gender, photo, tennis level, formats, bio, courts, availability, city, and preferences.
- Approximate location when permission is granted. We use it to calculate distance and do not show exact coordinates.
- Swipes, matches, messages, blocks, and reports needed to provide and protect the service.
- Push tokens and limited product events such as onboarding completion and match creation. Product events never contain message text, phone numbers, or precise location.

## Why we use data

We use data to authenticate players, find compatible nearby partners, create mutual matches, deliver chat and notifications, prevent abuse, investigate reports, and understand whether the service works.

## Sharing

Compatible authenticated players can see safe profile fields. Swipes stay private unless interest is mutual. We use Supabase, an SMS provider, Expo push services, Apple, and Google as processors needed to operate the app. We do not sell personal data.

## Retention and control

Players can edit their profile, disable notifications, block or report another player, unmatch, and delete their account in the app. Account deletion removes the profile, matches, messages, and related records. Safety reports may require a limited legal retention period, which the operator must define before launch.

## Security and age

Database row-level policies limit profile, match, and message access. Sensitive server credentials are never shipped in the app. Côte Tennis is for adults aged 18 or older.
